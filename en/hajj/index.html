Rules of Hajj

Hajj is the fifth pillar of Islam, obligatory once in a lifetime for every able Muslim.

Pillars of Hajj

  • Ihram with intention for Hajj
  • Standing at Arafah (greatest pillar)
  • Tawaf al-Ifadah
  • Sa'i between Safa & Marwa

Obligatory Acts

  • Ihram from the Miqat
  • Standing at Arafah until sunset
  • Spending night at Muzdalifah
  • Stoning the Jamarat
  • Shaving or shortening the hair
  • Spending nights at Mina
  • Farewell Tawaf

Types of Hajj

  • Ifrad: Ihram for Hajj only
  • Qiran: Combining Hajj and Umrah in one Ihram
  • Tamattu': Umrah then Hajj — the best

Hajj Day by Day

  1. ٨

    Day of Tarwiyah — 8 Dhul Hijjah

    Put on Ihram for Hajj in Mecca, travel to Mina, spend the night there.

  2. ٩

    Day of Arafah — 9 Dhul Hijjah

    Stand at Arafah from noon until sunset with abundant dua and dhikr, then proceed to Muzdalifah.

  3. ١٠

    Day of Sacrifice — Eid Day

    Stone the Jamrat al-Aqabah, sacrifice, shave or trim hair, perform Tawaf al-Ifadah and Sa'i.

  4. ١١

    Days of Tashreeq — 11, 12 & 13

    Spend nights in Mina, stone the three Jamarat after noon. Those in a hurry may leave on the 12th.

  5. Farewell Tawaf

    Last act before leaving Mecca. Exempt for women in menstruation or postpartum.
